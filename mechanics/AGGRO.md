#mechanics #design 
___

The enemies goals are split in two: Attack the player or Destroy the Egg.
#### Egg Aggro (EA)
As the name suggests: **EA** are enemies whom main goal is to damage the egg. They may inflict damage to the player on contact.
#### Player Aggro (PA)
PA enemies will actively chase and attack the player. They can inflict damage on contact or shoot projectiles.
